a little about me

I care about how software feels to the people using it.

I studied for my BSc at the University of Malawi, then trained as a software engineer with Holberton School and ALX Africa. Since then I've built software for schools, hospitals, businesses and marketplaces, from a school management system to medical software and a gig platform in Sweden.

These days I'm a software engineer at Niveosystems, working from Zomba. What I enjoy most is the moment a real person uses something I built and it simply works for them.
